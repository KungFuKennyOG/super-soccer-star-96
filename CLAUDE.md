# CLAUDE.md

Read this first in every session. Super Soccer Star '96 is an arcade VR soccer game for Meta Quest 3 (WebXR, Quest browser), hosted on GitHub Pages from `main`. It is a separate project from Last Call (KungFuKennyOG/last-call-vr); `engine/` started as a copy of Last Call's engine modules.

The owner is Kenny. He plays every change on a Quest 3, so make each change easy to check (see "Asking for a headset check"). Keep replies short and direct. He prefers honest answers over hedged ones.

## Layout

| Path | What goes there |
| --- | --- |

| `engine/` | Stage (renderer, rig), WebXR session, controllers and desktop input, frame loop (copied from Last Call) |
| `rules/` | **Pure game rules, no three.js**: tuning numbers, ball physics, passing, shooting, headers/volleys/bicycle kicks (`aerial.js`), AI (`ai.js` basics, `brain.js` levels and teammates finding space, `keeper.js`), the sim, match scoring, what the commentator says (`commentary.js`), the dance, training drills (`tutorial.js`), teams and kits (`teams.js`), cup runs (`cup.js`), weather (`weather.js`). Unit tested in Node. |
| `game/` | Everything that draws or sounds: PS1 materials, textures, stadium, skinned players (`body.js`, `head.js`, `hair.js`, `atlas.js`, `actors.js`, `squad.js`), HUD, menus (`menus.js`), trophy lift (`trophy.js`), training card (`training.js`), dance (`celebration.js`), commentator (`commentary.js`, voice clips in `assets/voice`), goal replays (`replay.js`), rain and snow (`weather.js`), full-time screens (`results.js`), audio and music, controls and intents (`controls.js`, `intent.js`), `main.js` |
| `tests/unit/` | Node tests for every module in `rules/`, plus `bot.js` (a scripted attacker) and `balance.test.js` (every level stays beatable) |

| `tools/` | `serve.mjs` local no-cache server, `lab.html` model lineup, `build-voice.py` renders the commentator's lines |

## How it fits together

- `rules/sim.js` owns the match: `stepSim(world, intent, dt)` takes one intent per frame (move, facing, shot, pass, knock, drag, call, strike, head) and returns events (`kick`, `pass`, `received`, `goal`, `saved`, `post`, `out`, `tackled` and so on). The page never moves the ball or players itself.
- `rules/match.js` turns events into points and banners and runs the 2-minute clock.
- `game/main.js` reads controls, builds the intent (`game/intent.js`: head-aim shooting, pass ring holds, your head for headers), steps the sim, records it for replays, then poses models and HUD.
- All feel numbers live in `rules/tuning.js`.

## Rules

- Plain ES modules, no bundler, no runtime npm packages. three.js is pinned to **r149** from jsDelivr via the import map.
- Game rules stay pure (no three, no DOM) so they're testable. Every new rules module gets a test file.
- Keep files under about 400 lines.
- `npm run check` must pass before committing. Run `npm run smoke` when a page changes.
- PS1 look: use `ps1()` from `game/ps1.js` for every material (vertex snap, affine textures, dither). Its strength is tunable in `PS1` (snap grid, color levels, dither amount); Kenny asked for it softer than the real PS1. Textures are painted in code at small sizes with nearest filtering.
- Kits are colors only: no real crests, sponsors, club or player names.

## Quest 3 budgets

72 fps minimum. Each player is one draw call (a SkinnedMesh). The headset renders at framebuffer scale 0.7.

## Decision log

- Name: Super Soccer Star '96. 90s logo and pixel-font UI.
- Comfort isn't a constraint (Kenny's call: it's just for him). Smooth turning is fine.
- Controls: sticky-ball dribbling, head-aimed shooting, timing-ring passes (hold, release when the ring meets the circle).
- First mode: 3 v 3 plus keeper on a half pitch, 2-minute arcade score attack.
- Cups (Kenny's format): every match is a comeback scenario (start behind, short clock, reach the target). Opponents never attack in play; they can score on the counter when you lose the ball (`counterAttack` in `rules/match.js`). World Cup '96 = 32 nations, groups of 4 with draws then knockouts. Champions Cup = 16 city clubs, knockouts. 3 arcade continues per cup for knockout losses; going out in the groups is final.
- Teams (agreed list): 32 nations in `rules/teams.js`; 16 clubs by city name (London Red and London Blue both in, Marseille out). No club names or crests.
- Menus are in-world panels for both headset and browser. Picking is A/X or Enter, never a trigger (a held trigger would fire a shot at kick-off). Shoot and pass also ignore input until both triggers have been released after kick-off.
- Winning a cup ends with a physical trophy lift: grab with grips, raise above your head.
- Team stars matter on the pitch (`rules/skill.js`, `tuneFor(yours, theirs)`): their stars scale pressing, tackling, interceptions and keeper; yours scale teammates' pace and passing and a little of your pace and shooting. Max ±10% between 1 and 5 stars (15% compounded too hard).
- World Cup team select is a world map: the stick moves to the nearest nation that way; European picks zoom to Europe.
- PS1 wobble is a setting (OFF / LOW / FULL, default LOW) on the settings screen. In the headset LOW is a light vertex wobble with no texture warp (warp swims as your head moves); FULL adds the warp back.
- Match length is a setting (SHORT 0.6x, NORMAL, LONG 1.5x, EPIC 2.5x): Score Attack's 2 minutes and every cup scenario's clock scale with it. Settings persist in localStorage.
- The keeper always faces the ball in a crouched ready stance and side-steps instead of running; he hugs the ball after a catch and eases into position (no twitching). Keeper animation lives in `game/keeperpose.js` (Oct 2026 redo, Kenny asked for better ones): the sim sets `gk.save { y, kind, id }` and `gk.dive` (-1/0/1; 0 is a standing save for shots within `KEEPER.standSave` of him); the page latches it and plays a load, a dive flat (low) or up and across (high) with hands leading, a catch gathered into the chest or a stiff-wristed parry, landing on his side and up through one knee. Standing saves scoop, chest or catch overhead. A split step on every strike, looser when the ball's far. `tools/lab.html?pose=dive&y=..&save=..&times=..` freezes moments side by side.
- Passes lob over defenders in the lane, otherwise go along the ground (`rules/pass.js` choosePass). The lane is a corridor that widens toward the receiver (`PASS.lobNear`/`lobFar` in tuning). A blue ring and a LOB tag show it before you pass.
- On a hat trick (your 3rd, 6th... goal): a rhythm dance mini-game (`rules/dance.js`, `game/celebration.js`) at 88 bpm, one move per beat. Teammates face you and mirror the moves.
- Right stick is only for turning. Skills are on buttons: A knock-on (standing still: flick-up), B drag-back, Y restart.
- Your own body in first person shows only boots and lower socks, plus low-poly hands on the controller grips (`game/hands.js`): open by default, pointing while you squeeze the grip, a fist on the trigger, sweatband in your kit. They follow the WebXR grip-space spec (origin mid-fist, -Z along the handle toward the thumb, palm toward -X on the right hand, +Y back to the wrist).
- Trophies are deliberately PS1: few sides, flat shading, dithered metal textures, engraved plates. While you grip a hand-hold, a posed hand on the cup replaces your controller hand; each hand takes its own side (left hand on the cup's left).
- Every club has its own ground (`game/grounds.js` recipes: stand tiers, corners, roof, lights, time of day, mowing, seat colors, extras like Liverpool's single big end, Dortmund's wall, Turin's track, Milan's towers; the two Milan clubs share a ground). Champions Cup alternates: home in R16 and SF, away in QF and the final. Nations and the World Cup use the national stadium.
- The crowd is ~10k instanced fan dots in both teams' colors; they bob all game, yours jump when your team scores, theirs when they score on the counter (vertex shader, one draw call).
- Skill moves (`rules/skills.js`, gestures in `rules/gestures.js`, setting SKILL MOVES OFF / GESTURE / GRIP + GESTURE, default GESTURE): step-over (right-hand sideways flick: fakes that way, you burst the other), roulette (left hand, any sharp flick or a quick circle: spin away, shielded; it moved to the left hand because a right-hand circle's upward part read as a rainbow), nutmeg (right-hand jab forward-down: through the legs of a man square in front, he's stunned and you collect it beyond him) and rainbow flick (right-hand upward flick: over his head, run onto it). Gestures are sharp hand flicks relative to your head in your body's frame (1.9 m/s+), only on the ball with a defender within 5 m, not while charging a shot (right) or holding the pass ring (left), 0.6 s cooldown; in GRIP + GESTURE mode the right grip is the clutch for both hands; Kenny plays with his hands at his sides so no grip is needed by default. Browser keys 1-4. A presser falls for the same move twice in a row (SKILL.bite), then reads it; mixing moves keeps working. Style points per skill; a goal after 2+ skills in the attack is JOGA BONITA (+300 each). Each move has a training drill with a live presser.
- Ultras behind the goal (`game/ultras.js`, built by `grounds.js`): the home end's lower tier centre-left is a packed curva in the home side's colors that bounces all game (crowd shader `aUltra`); the away block at the right end has its own. Big flags on poles (4 painted designs per side in kit colors, instanced, a vertex shader swings them on the pole and ripples the cloth, harder when that side scores) and banners draped over the front railing with generic words (FORZA, ULTRAS, CURVA...). Flares with smoke were tried and dropped (Kenny).
- Music: original chiptune tracks sequenced in `game/music.js` (menu, dance).
- Players are code-built low-poly skinned meshes measured against Kenny's FIFA 98 / Virtua Striker 2 screenshots (`game/body.js`, measurements in `P`): realistic athletic male proportions (1.82 m, about 8 heads), broad shoulders, straight torso and narrow hips (no waist, or they read as women), a thick neck carried forward, shirt tucked into long wide 90s shorts, socks to the knee, big calves, chunky boots. The face is sculpted in the geometry (`game/head.js`: brow ridge, deep-set eyes, nose, muzzle, chin, cheekbones, jaw corners). Textures are never flat: blotchy photo-like cloth with folds, skin tone variation and painted muscle light, V-necks, raglan shoulder stripes (`game/atlas.js`, 256 sheet). Mostly short hair (`game/hair.js`), most players stubbled. Gouraud shading, baked ambient occlusion, extra contrast (`ps1({ pop: true })`). Chunky stylised proportions and a realistic CC0 rigged model (Quaternius) were both tried and rejected. `tools/lab.html` shows a lineup for checking models.
- Aerial play (`rules/aerial.js`, numbers in `AIR`): headers use your real head (position and speed from the headset) and aim themselves: within 22 m of goal (`AIR.headAuto`) they always go on target, where you look if that's at the goal, else a corner away from the keeper (Kenny: aiming with your head in VR is too hard); further out they're a flick-on the way you face; pull the trigger on a dropping ball for a volley, or look up and pull it for a bicycle kick (always on target, slow motion). Teammates cross it in when you're in the box; A while standing flicks the ball up. A dropping ball isn't trapped above waist height, so it can be headed or volleyed. Goal bonuses: header +500, volley +600, bicycle +1500.
- Commentator (setting OFF / TEXT / VOICE, default VOICE): a British TV voice, pre-recorded lines in `game/assets/voice` (Kokoro TTS voice bm_george, rendered by `tools/build-voice.py` from hand-written phonemes in `lines.json`; add a line there and re-run it). Subtitles float just below your view. `rules/commentary.js` picks lines by event and priority.
- Calling for the ball: raise either hand above your head (fist 14 cm above eye level; lower it to call again), or the pass button without the ball. A two-note whistle, a buzz in that hand, and the arrow over the carrier pops. A called teammate passes to you (or crosses if you're in the box).
- Training mode (`rules/tutorial.js` drills, `game/training.js` card): 19 drills in order, each sets up the pitch (`setupDrill`: defenders parked and pinned via `world.drill.frozen`, teammates can hold the ball until called via `mateHold`, crosses forced to head height via `crossHead`) and counts progress from sim events. In the headset the call drill only counts a raised hand. X / N skips a drill, Y / R resets it. No replays, dance or clock in training. Add a move to the game → add a drill for it.
- Hit effects / juice (setting HIT EFFECTS OFF / ON, `game/juice.js`): hit-stop on your strikes (the sim steps with dt 0 for 35-90 ms by power; not on the bicycle, which has slow motion), power-scaled haptics, the ball swells on contact, turf bits kick up, a streak behind hard shots, the crowd draws breath when a shot is on target, a haptic heartbeat on your goals. Cosmetic apart from the hit-stop.
- AI (Oct 2026 rehaul, Kenny: the first AI felt better, wanted it more challenging with better teammates). `rules/ai.js` basics, `rules/brain.js` level and teammate support, numbers in `AI` / `BRAIN` / `PASS` in tuning.
  - Defenders work as a unit: the man nearest the carrier comes at him from the goal side (`pressGap`, reading his run with `pressLead`) and goes in for the ball inside `pressIn`; attackers within `markDanger` of goal (and always the most dangerous one) are marked goal-side; a defender with nobody worth marking covers between the ball and goal (`coverGap`). A pass in flight isn't chased: the defender nearest its receiver closes him from the far side of the ball, so only a man already in the lane can cut it out.
  - Teammates look for space (`mateSupport`): candidate spots scored for room, a clear lane from the ball, their role (the runner beyond you toward the box, the support man ~8.5 m from you level or behind) and not crowding; they stick to a spot unless a clearly better one turns up (`RUN.stick`). On the ball (`mateDecision`) they shoot when close or with a clear sight of goal, give it back when you call (but not a short pass straight into a defender, and not while a man is right on you: a touch first), pass when you're better placed and open, and carry it into space away from pressure (`mateDribbleTarget`). Their shots go to the far side from the keeper.
  - Passes to you lead you at most `PASS.leadYou` (3.5 m; long leads used to roll into the net), teammates only loft it to you if someone's inside `PASS.mateLane`, and nothing under `PASS.lobMin` (8 m) is lofted (short lobs hung and got cut out).
  - Keeper a bit harder (react 0.17 s, reach 1.04): a low corner from 17 m goes in ~45%, a high one ~70%.
  - Levels (`BRAIN`, `levelFor`: cup group/R16 0, QF 1, SF 2, final 3; Score Attack 0, or 1 against 5 stars) ask a little more each round, never a wall (Kenny: you're the superstar): tighter ball-side marking (`markTight`), a sharper keeper (`gkSharp`), the keeper narrowing and sweeping (`gkOut`, `gkRush`), drag-backs read sooner (`dragBite`), runners tracked closer (`runMargin`). Difficulty comes from behaviour, not more speed. Cup deficits are at most 2 goals with 85 + 45 s per goal down (`scenarioFor`). `balance.test.js` runs `bot.js` (dribbles, passes when closed down, calls for one-twos, reads passes to it) at every level: ~0.45 goals per attack in the group stage, ~0.3 in the final (it was ~0.6 before the rehaul). The bot is touchy about where the spare man stands (`coverGap` 5 m sat in its passing lanes and halved its goals; 4.5 m doesn't), so tune that one in the headset, not by the bot.
  - Nobody flips jobs at a threshold: `defenderTargets` gets last step's jobs (`w.brain.jobs`): the presser keeps going unless another man is 1 m nearer, a marker keeps his man unless another is 2 m nearer, a marked man stays marked 1.5 m past `markDanger`, and the presser backs off 0.6 m further out than he goes in. A pass that dies short of its man (slow and 3 m+ away, or 2.5 s old) turns back into a loose ball someone chases.
  - Dropped: reading where you look (READ!), doubling up near the box and the line stepping up (they freed a teammate and made later rounds easier), offside (a hassle in 3 v 3).
  - Drag-backs: the presser falls for `dragBite` in a row (3, or 2 in the final), then reads it (NOT AGAIN banner, no stumble).
  - Keeper (`rules/keeper.js`): one on one with nobody between you and goal he comes off his line (further at higher levels); a quick tap (`SHOT.chipBelow`) is a chip that drops over him (+400 CHIP). From level 1 he races out to claim ground passes played into space that he can reach first; never lobs, crosses or your own dribbling touches and knock-ons. He's checked before the ball moves each step so he's never a frame late. He can't gather his own parry for 0.6 s (the rebound is live), and once you've gone past him he can't save. The ball is drawn in his gloves whenever he has it (`holdInGloves` in main.js).
  - Passes: the receiver gets first touch unless a defender is 0.4 m closer (0.9 m for a pass to you; `runMargin` for the runner); a lofted ball on its way up can't be intercepted.
- Goal replays (setting OFF / ON): the last ~4 s before a goal replay with copies of the players, slowing for the finish, filmed from a touchline gantry into a render target (256x144, every third frame in the headset) shown on a floating CRT in front of you and on the stadium's big screen. A or B skips. When rendering into it, restore the previous render target afterwards (in WebXR that's the headset's framebuffer; setting null dropped frames and made the view judder). No replay on a hat trick (the dance goes instead).

- Menu (Oct 2026): SCORE ATTACK, WORLD CUP, CHAMPIONS CUP, TRAINING, SETTINGS. Settings have their own screen with the locker under them (ball: CLASSIC/STAR/RETRO/GOLD, boots: BLACK/WHITE/RED/GOLD for your first-person boots and the replay you, BIG HEADS: head bone x1.65 on everyone), all options always available.
- Weather (setting RANDOM / CLEAR / RAIN / SNOW, random is 65/23/12): `rules/weather.js` puts a surface on the ball (rain: skids on, snow: heavy, dead bounce), `game/weather.js` draws streaks or flakes in a box round your head (one draw call), `stadium.setWeather` darkens or snows over the pitch and greys the fog. Snow brings out the orange ball (unless you picked a locker ball). Training is always clear.
- Keeper modes were built and scrapped (Oct 2026): penalty shootouts (you taking kicks and saving theirs with amplified gloves, also after level cup knockouts) and KEEPER RUSH (you in goal facing waves of shots). Kenny: not good, not worth the bloat. Level knockouts still just need the win. Don't bring them back; the keeper animations from that work stayed.
- Challenges (feats that unlocked the locker) and the "scouting report" banner were built and dropped (Kenny). Don't bring them back.
- In the headset never write `camera.position` / `rotation` in the frame (the renderer writes the real head pose in during render; code before that reads it).
- More commentator lines: variety for common events and weather kick-offs.
